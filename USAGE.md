# NanoClaw brand usage

Use the project name **NanoClaw** when referring to the open-source project. For its README, documentation, and project artwork, use the supplied assets and follow the sizing, spacing, colour, and typography examples in [BRAND.md](BRAND.md).

Choose the light or dark logo to match its background. Keep the mascot and wordmark together in their supplied proportions. For a square surface, use the app icon. Describe third-party projects and integrations clearly so readers can identify their publisher.

The bundled Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts include their [license files](assets/fonts/). For questions about using the name or artwork outside these examples, open an issue in this repository.
